HIIT 974 is an offline HIIT / interval training timer. No account, no login, no
credentials, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no user-generated content.

No permission prompts: the app requests no access to location, contacts, camera, photos,
microphone, health data, or App Tracking Transparency.

No external services and no network access at all: no URLSession, no WKWebView, no
third-party SDK, no analytics, no ads, no backend, no AI service. Apple system
frameworks only (SwiftUI, SwiftData, Swift Charts, AVFoundation). The app works fully in
airplane mode. All data is stored locally on device and never leaves it.

Functions identically in all regions. No regulated-industry content, no health claims,
no HealthKit, no third-party intellectual property.

AUDIO AND BACKGROUND EXECUTION: audio cues are short tones played only while the app is
in the foreground. UIBackgroundModes is not declared and no MediaPlayer / Now Playing
integration is present. The app plays nothing in the background or on the lock screen,
by design, following the 2.5.4 feedback on version 1.0. The workout screen keeps the
display awake so the timer stays visible.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.6: the two workouts force-created on first launch ("Tabata", "HIIT Full
Body") are gone. A "Suggestions du chef" folder now offers five ready-made workouts (the
original two plus three new ones); the user adds one with a single tap, nothing is
created automatically. Existing installs that had the two old defaults, untouched, get
them removed once on first launch after the update — a renamed or edited workout is
never touched. No new capability, data, or permission change, and still no background
audio.

HOW TO TEST: no setup required. The workouts list is empty on first launch. At the top
of the "Séances" (Workouts) tab, tap the "Suggestions du chef" row (fork-and-knife icon)
to open five ready-made workouts, then tap any row, e.g. "Tabata" (20 s/10 s, 8
exercises) or "HIIT Full Body" (40 s/20 s, 5 exercises, 3 rounds) — to add it to your
workouts.

Tap the workout row you just added, in the "Séances" tab, to open the timer, then tap the
central play button. The screen turns orange for a 10-second "Préparation" (get ready)
countdown, then red for the first work interval; rest is blue and between-round recovery
is teal. The bottom controls are previous phase, play/pause and skip; the X at the top
right closes the timer. Beeps mark the last three seconds of each segment and the
halfway point of work.

Tap "+" to create a workout, set the durations and counts, then tap "Enregistrer"
(Save). Inside the editor, "Choisir les exercices" (Choose exercises) opens a menu to
name each exercise, either from the built-in list or by typing.

To edit an existing workout: swipe a row from left to right, or swipe from right to left
and tap the orange "Modifier" (Edit), or long-press the row. Note that a full right-to-
left swipe deletes the workout. The "Historique" (History) tab shows completed workouts,
an activity chart and totals.

LANGUAGE: the interface is French-only. Key terms — Séances = Workouts, Historique =
History, Préparation = Get ready, Travail = Work, Repos = Rest, Réinit. du round = Round
recovery, Choisir les exercices = Choose exercises, Récents = Recently used, Effort =
Work phase, Récupération = Recovery phase, Ensuite = Up next, Séance terminée ! =
Workout complete!, Aucune séance enregistrée = No workouts recorded yet.

This release (1.6) was verified on iOS Simulator only (fresh install and upgrade from a
1.5 store with the two old default workouts). It does not touch audio, background
execution, or permissions — the iPhone 16 Pro / iOS 26.6 device verification from the
1.5 submission still applies there. iPhone-only, portrait-only, minimum iOS 26.4.
