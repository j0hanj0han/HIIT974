Give every exercise in your workouts a name.

• Name your exercises — "Burpees", "Plank", "Squats"… The name is shown in large type during the workout, in place of the generic work label.
• Pick from the list or write your own — a catalogue of 40+ exercises sorted by category, and any name you type becomes reusable across your other workouts.
• During rest, you already see the next exercise — enough time to get into position before it starts again.

Naming is optional: your existing workouts behave exactly as before.
