Your exercises finally have names.

• Name every exercise in a workout — "Burpees", "Plank", "Lunges"… The name shows in large type while you work, in place of the generic work label. No more trying to remember what comes next.

• Pick from the catalogue or type your own — 42 exercises sorted into five categories: cardio, upper body, lower body, core, mobility.

• Your own names come back on their own — type a name once and it turns up under "Recent" the next time you build a workout.

• During rest, you already see what's coming — the "Up next" line announces it, so you have time to get into position.

All of this is optional: your existing workouts run exactly as before.
